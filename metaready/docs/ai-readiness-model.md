# AI-readiness model

## Principle

Information is not “AI ready” in the abstract. It can be fit or unfit for a specific use case under specific controls.

## Use-case templates

- Enterprise search.
- Retrieval-augmented knowledge assistant.
- Analytical reporting.
- Classification or routing support.
- Agent tool source.

## Dimensions

1. Accountability.
2. Purpose fit.
3. Source authority.
4. Currency.
5. Access control.
6. Privacy and lawful use.
7. Machine readability.
8. Terminology.
9. Identifier stability.
10. Quality evidence.
11. Lifecycle control.
12. Permitted AI use.
13. Human oversight.

## Result structure

Every dimension shows:

- result;
- score;
- evidence;
- confidence;
- assumption;
- recommended action; and
- accountable role.

## Overall status

- **Ready:** strong evidence, no blockers and limited warnings.
- **Ready with controls:** usable only after stated controls or remediation.
- **Not ready:** material blockers or low overall evidence.
- **Not assessed:** reserved for assets without a completed assessment.

The arithmetic average is visible as a navigation aid. It does not override blockers and is not legal, security or model approval.
