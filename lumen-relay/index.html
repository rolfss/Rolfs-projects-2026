Field manual

How to play

  1. Collect a fragment.Move through a glowing circle, triangle, or square. You can carry one at a time.
  2. Find its matching gate.Deliver the fragment to the same symbol at the edge of the arena.
  3. Avoid interference.Contact costs integrity, breaks your chain, and drops the fragment.
  4. Use dash deliberately.Press Space or the Dash button. You are protected for a brief moment, followed by a cooldown.
