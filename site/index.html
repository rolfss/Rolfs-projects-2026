MetaReady

Information Governance & AI-Readiness Workbench

MetaReady answers a practical question: is this information trustworthy enough to share, analyze, search or use in an AI system?

It connects metadata, ownership, provenance, sensitivity, retention, lineage, quality and use-case-specific AI readiness—then turns gaps into an explainable remediation backlog.
