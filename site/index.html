It connects metadata, ownership, provenance, sensitivity, retention, lineage, quality and use-case-specific AI readiness—then turns gaps into an explainable remediation backlog.

Lumen Relay

A compact signal-routing arcade game.

Collect glowing fragments, deliver them to matching gates and dash through a field that becomes faster and denser with every wave.

Each run lasts 90 seconds. Shape-coded signals, generated sound, responsive Canvas rendering and touch controls keep the game readable across devices.
